Youth Survey Preview

Youth Survey Preview

30 questions across 10 topics. About 8-10 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers, and you can skip any question.

For staff: preview for piloting wording, length and reading level. Answers typed here are not saved. Grey lines under each question show its ID, source and any national benchmark; remove those lines from the youth-facing version.

Supportive adults

These questions are about adults in your life outside your family.

1. Outside of your family, is there at least one adult in your school, neighborhood or community who knows you well and who you can rely on for advice or guidance?
SA1 · Adapted to youth self-report from NSCH 2024 item K9Q96 (parent report) National benchmark: NSCH 2024
2. How many adults outside your family could you go to for advice about school or your future?
SA2 · Original

Bridging ties

These questions are about people and opportunities you know about.

3. Do you know an adult outside your family who works in a job you might like to have someday?
SA3 · Original (occupational exposure through networks)
4. In the past 3 months, has an adult outside your family helped you find an opportunity, such as a program, job, internship, scholarship or class?
SA4 · Original (social capital mobilization)
5. How comfortable would you feel asking an adult you don't know well (for example, a teacher, coach or someone at a workplace) for advice or help?
SA5 · Original (help-seeking across social distance)

RQ2 · Original Program youth only
8. My mentor helps me think about my future.
RQ3 · Original Program youth only
9. My mentor connects me with people or opportunities I would not have found on my own.
RQ4 · Original (bridging function of mentoring) Program youth only
10. I look forward to meeting with my mentor.
RQ5 · Original Program youth only

School belonging

How much do you agree with each statement about your school?

11. I feel close to people at my school.
BE1 · CDC Youth Risk Behavior Survey 2023 (Q103) exact wording National benchmark: YRBS 2023
12. I feel like I belong at my school.
BE2 · Similar to PISA sense-of-belonging items (wording adapted) National benchmark: PISA 2022 US
13. People at my school notice when I am good at something.
BE3 · Similar to Psychological Sense of School Membership scale (Goodenow 1993); wording adapted
14. I feel left out of things at my school.
BE4 · Similar to PISA sense-of-belonging items (wording adapted)

Self-efficacy

How sure are you that you can do each of these things?

15. How sure are you that you can finish your schoolwork even when it is hard?
SE1 · Original, following Bandura (2006) guide for constructing self-efficacy scales
16. How sure are you that you can ask a teacher for help when you need it?
SE2 · Original (Bandura 2006 approach)
17. How sure are you that you can get the grades you want this year?
SE3 · Original (Bandura 2006 approach)
18. How sure are you that you can find information about college or job training after high school?
SE4 · Original (future/navigational self-efficacy)
19. How sure are you that you can start a conversation with an adult you don't know well about something you care about?
SE5 · Original (bridging self-efficacy)
20. How sure are you that you can solve a problem if you try hard enough?
SE6 · Original, in the spirit of general self-efficacy measures
